The Festival of Design is Kosovo's most significant cultural design event. Each year it transforms Pristina into a stage for design thinking, making, and dialogue, drawing practitioners from across the region and world.

The festival operates across multiple venues, from galleries and public squares to industrial spaces. Every edition is built around a central theme that responds to Kosovo's evolving cultural moment.

What It Includes

  • 01

    Public exhibitions across Pristina's cultural venues

  • 02

    International speaker programme and keynote talks

  • 03

    Open workshops for students and emerging practitioners

  • 04

    Collaborative installations with international partners

  • 05

    Shop Design market pop-up during festival week
